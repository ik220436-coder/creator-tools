Complete Guide to Instagram Image Dimensions

Posting images with incorrect aspect ratios or sub-optimal resolutions results in harsh compression, awkward automatic feed crops, and blurry rendering on mobile retina displays. Instagram's ingestion pipeline aggressively compresses photos that exceed its dimensional boundaries, while images that are too small get stretched and lose crispness.

Our Instagram Size Converter processes your image locally using standard HTML5 Canvas high-fidelity bicubic interpolation. You have full control over the composition: choose between full-frame cropping with customizable focal centers, or letterbox padding using a stylish blurred background.

Why 1080px Width is the Gold Standard

Instagram displays feed images at a maximum width of 1080 pixels. By exporting your photos at exactly 1080px wide with our optimized 90–95% quality setting, you bypass Instagram's server-side recompression algorithm, preserving maximum sharpness and color fidelity.

The Power of Portrait 4:5 (1080 × 1350)

Vertical 4:5 posts take up approximately 33% more vertical screen real-estate in the mobile feed than standard 1:1 squares. This increased screen footprint boosts thumb-stopping power, engagement, and dwell time on your content.

Instagram Size Converter FAQ

Which format should I choose: JPG, PNG, or WebP?

For standard photos and portraits, JPG (Quality 90–92%) is recommended by Instagram for best compatibility and fast uploads. For illustrations, digital art, or typography with sharp edges, choose PNG.

How does "Fit & Pad" with blurred background work?

When you don't want any portion of your image cropped out (e.g. wide landscape photos intended for a 1:1 or 4:5 post), "Fit & Pad" scales the entire image into the frame and fills the negative space with a soft, aesthetically pleasing blurred replica of your photo.

What is the ideal resolution for Instagram Stories and Reels?

Both Stories and Reels require a 9:16 vertical aspect ratio with standard dimensions of 1080 × 1920 pixels. Keep critical text and logos within the safe center zone to prevent them from being obscured by user handles or interface buttons.

PDF Page Organizer FAQ

Are my confidential PDF documents safe?

Yes. Our application uses Mozilla's open-source PDF.js and pdf-lib. All byte parsing, canvas rendering, and PDF generation occur in your browser's private memory sandbox. No remote server ever receives your document bytes.

Does rearranging pages affect searchable text and vector graphics?

No. Because we use pdf-lib for structural page copying, original vector lines, embedded fonts, and selectable text streams are preserved untouched in the generated PDF.

What is the maximum file size or page limit?

Because processing occurs on your device, capacity is determined by your system's RAM. Most modern devices comfortably handle PDFs up to several hundred pages and 100MB+ in file size.

Maximizing Video SEO with Formatted Descriptions

Your YouTube video description is one of the primary signals that YouTube and Google search algorithms use to index your content. A haphazardly formatted description wastes valuable keyword equity and confuses both viewers and search spiders.

Google Search Rich Chapter Snippets

When you include compliant timestamps (starting at 00:00 with at least 3 chapters of 10+ seconds duration), Google's search engine displays your video with interactive chapter scrub bars right on google.com search results, driving massive organic traffic.

The Above-the-Fold 2-Line Hook

Only the first 100–120 characters are visible before the viewer clicks "...more". Place your primary value proposition and call to action in those opening sentences rather than burying links under massive text blocks.

YouTube Description Formatter FAQ

Why is 00:00 mandatory for YouTube chapters?

YouTube's automated chaptering system strictly requires the first chapter to be set to 00:00 (or 0:00). If the first timestamp starts at any other second (such as 00:15), YouTube will treat timestamps as plain text and will not generate interactive scrub bar chapters.

What is YouTube's maximum character limit?

YouTube allows up to 5,000 characters per description. Our editor provides a live character progress bar that turns red if you approach or exceed this hard constraint.
